# Executive Diagnostic: Retail Performance & Product Strategy Analysis

**Client:** Retail Operations & Strategy Team  
**Analysis Period:** January 1, 2016 – February 20, 2021  
**Dataset Scope:** 62,884 Transactions | 15,266 Customers | 2,517 Products | 67 Stores | 5 Currencies  
**Status:** Completed  

---

## 1. Executive Summary

Over the analyzed 5-year period, the business generated **$55.7M in global net sales** with a robust **gross profit margin of 58.6%**. Sales experienced aggressive expansion from 2016 ($6.9M) to a peak in 2019 ($18.3M), followed by a sharp **49.2% contraction in 2020 ($9.3M)** due to physical retail exposure (in-store accounts for 79.5% of revenue).

Key strategic vulnerabilities and growth vectors identified:
1. **Catalog Bloat:** An 80/20 Pareto skew where **28.4% of SKUs (708 products) generate 80% of revenue**, while **32.3% of SKUs (805 products)** qualify for immediate rationalization or markdown.
2. **Channel Imbalance:** E-commerce accounts for only **20.5% of revenue**, leaving the business vulnerable to in-store foot traffic disruptions.
3. **Store Footprint Inefficiency:** Smaller footprint stores (e.g., Wyoming at 840 m²) achieve **$1,453.53/m² in sales density**, outperforming larger retail units by up to 60%.

## 2. Strategic Diagnostic by Business Question

### 2.1 Revenue Drivers & Margins
* **Top Revenue Generators:** Premium Desktop PCs dominate the top 10 products. The single largest revenue driver is the *WWI Desktop PC2.33 X2330 Black* ($505,450 revenue across 550 units), followed by the *Adventure Works Desktop PC2.33 XD233* series.
* **Margin Health:** Desktop PCs yield a uniform **66.87% gross margin**, delivering over $300k in net profit per SKU.
* **Category Contribution:** **Computers ($19.4M)** and **Home Appliances ($10.8M)** contribute over 54% of global revenue. *Games and Toys* ($0.8M) represents less than 2% of total turnover.

### 2.2 Store Optimization & Sales Density
* **Volume vs. Efficiency:** While the Northwest Territories (Store 9, $1.34M) and Washington DC (Store 64, $1.26M) lead in raw turnover, **Store 66 in Wyoming is the most efficient physical asset**, delivering **$1,453.53 in revenue per square meter**.
* **High-Density Cluster:** Top sales density stores are concentrated in compact regional formats: Alaska ($1,050.63/m²), Hawaii ($1,009.59/m²), and New Hampshire ($958.14/m²).
* **International Footprint:** Store 29 in Enna, Italy leads European physical operations at **$913.86/m²**.

### 2.3 Sales Trend & Seasonality
* **Historical Trajectory:** 
  * 2016: $6.9M | 2017: $7.4M (+7.2%) | 2018: $12.8M (+73.0%) | 2019: $18.3M (+43.0%)
  * 2020: $9.3M (-49.2%) — steep drop aligned with global COVID-19 retail lockdowns.
  * 2021: $1.0M (reflects partial data through February 20, 2021).
* **Seasonality:** Strong Q4 demand surge annually in November and December, followed by a Q1 inventory replenishment dip.

### 2.4 Underperforming Products & Delisting Candidates
* **32.3% of SKUs (805 products)** occupy the bottom quadrant (sub-median sales volume AND sub-median revenue).
* **Dead Stock Examples:**
  * *SV USB Data Cables* (Silver, Grey, Pink, Black): Generating less than $35 total revenue over 5 years.
  * *Litware Case Fans & Oscillating Fans*: Under $30 total sales with slow inventory turns.
* **Action:** 805 items should be placed on clearance markdown or phased out to liberate working capital.

### 2.5 Customer Purchasing Dynamics
* **Core Demographics:** Customers aged **35–49 ($22.4M)** and **50–64 ($18.1M)** generate the majority of total spend.
* **Customer Retention:** The brand exhibits a **55.4% repeat purchase rate**, with loyal customers averaging 2 to 4 lifetime orders.
* **Gender Distribution:** Revenue is evenly split (approx. 51% Male / 49% Female), with male purchasing tilting higher toward Computers and female toward Home Appliances.

---

## 3. Actionable Strategic Recommendations

1. **Rationalize Catalog (Prune 805 SKUs):**
   * Delist the bottom 805 accessory and fan SKUs currently generating <$100 lifetime sales.
   * Consolidate shelf space around the top 28.4% of SKUs that generate 80% of top-line revenue.

2. **Rebalance Retail Footprint toward Compact Formats:**
   * Replicate the 800–1,200 m² compact footprint modeled by Store 66 (Wyoming) and Store 43 (Alaska).
   * Evaluate underperforming large-format stores (>2,000 m²) for floor-space downsizing to increase sales density.

3. **Accelerate Online Channel Expansion:**
   * Scale digital marketing and e-commerce infrastructure to lift online share from **20.5% toward 35–40%**, mitigating vulnerability to in-store retail disruptions.

4. **Targeted Loyalty Programs for the 35–64 Demographic:**
   * Create VIP upgrade and trade-in cycles for high-margin Desktop PCs targeted at the core 35–64 age segment.
